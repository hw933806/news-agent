# Daily Stock News — 2026-10-09

## RKT — Rocket Companies
- **[Rocket Companies]** — Freddie Mac's 30-yr fixed mortgage rate rose to **7.40%** (week of Oct 8), up from 7.28% and its **7th straight weekly increase** — now ~110bp above a year ago (6.30%), with the 10-yr Treasury averaging 5.28%.
