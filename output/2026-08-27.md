# Daily Stock News — 2026-08-27

## CPNG — Coupang
- 🎯 **Regulatory / KFTC** — Coupang refused a Korea Fair Trade Commission on-site inspection into whether it shifted price-matching discount costs onto suppliers (a suspected Large Retail Business Act violation) and **sued the regulator** for skipping the 7-day advance notice it says the law requires. The KFTC tried four times, then withdrew empty-handed on Aug 24 — believed to be the **first inspection refusal under Korea's retail law since the KFTC was founded**. The agency now plans a fine and may pursue a criminal referral, while lawmakers move to close the inspection-notice loophole. A new, distinct regulatory front on top of the KRW 162.8B search-ranking fine appeal and the PIPC data-breach penalty — squarely on CPNG's regulatory-overhang concern. ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20260827/watchdogs-probe-into-coupang-stalls-after-inspection-refusal))
