## FERG — Ferguson Enterprises
- **Ferguson** — Reported Q2 FY26 this morning (first print since joining the S&P 500): net sales **$8.8B, +4.6% YoY** (organic +3.8%, acquisitions +1.0%), operating profit **$893M**. Board declared a $0.89/sh dividend (payable Oct 7).
