## CPNG — Coupang
- 🎯 **Coupang (CPNG)** — **South Korea's KFTC hit Coupang Eats with a provisional ~₩300.8B (~$220M) fine for most-favored-nation (MFN) conduct** — requiring partner restaurants to give Coupang Eats terms no worse than those on rival apps across Jul 2023–Jan 2026 (31 months). The regulator pegged MFN-related sales at **~₩5.0T** (vs Coupang's own ~₩710B estimate) and added **+20% for prior violations**; Baemin (Woowa) was fined ₩331.1B the same day (combined **>₩630B**). Coupang called it excessive ("600x+ past cases"), disputes the scope expansion to "total delivery sales," and signaled it will contest it; a separate **tying case (~₩200B expected)** remains pending. This is a core regulatory-overhang item — a fresh, large food-delivery hit distinct from the ₩162.8B search-ranking appeal and the PIPC breach fine. _Note: announced Mon Nov 9; it was not surfaced in the Nov 10/11 runs, so it is caught up here._ ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/baemin-coupang-face-463-million-in-fines/12962946), [The Star/Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/09/south-korea-fines-coupang-220-million-for-abusing-dominance-in-food-delivery-market))
