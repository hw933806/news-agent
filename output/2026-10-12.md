# Daily Stock News — 2026-10-12

## AUR — Aurora Innovation
- **Aurora (AUR)** — FMCSA granted a **five-year federal exemption** (DOT approved late Oct 7; Federal Register notice published Fri Oct 9) letting Aurora and any opted-in Level 4 carrier use **cab-mounted warning beacons** in place of manually placing roadside flares/triangles — removing a human-must-exit-the-cab requirement that was a structural blocker to true driver-out scaling, and replacing the four rolling 3-month waivers Aurora had operated under since Oct 2025 with a stable multi-year framework. A 7th Circuit challenge to the waiver was denied Oct 5; rivals Kodiak (KDK), Waabi and Stack AV have also opted in. ([CCJ](https://www.ccjdigital.com/regulations/article/15837000/court-shoots-down-operators-driverless-warningdevice-waiver-lawsuit-as-fmcsa-gives-5year-extension))
