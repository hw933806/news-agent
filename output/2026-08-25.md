## RKT — Rocket Companies
- **Redfin (Rocket)** — FTC settled its antitrust suit against Zillow and Rocket's Redfin hours before trial was to open: the two firms' rental-listing (ILS) partnership stays intact through at least 2030, Redfin keeps the disputed $100M and the Zillow-syndicated multifamily listings, but must independently re-enter rental-advertising sales within ~6 months; the companies pay ~$2M to reimburse state AGs. KBW called the terms favorable vs. more punitive outcomes and Redfin's re-entry impact "manageable" — a legal overhang on the Redfin integration clears.
