# Daily Stock News — 2026-08-31

## RKT — Rocket Companies
- **Rocket Companies** — Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote (Fri, Aug 28) landed clearly hawkish, warning that "underlying inflation" hasn't improved enough; fed funds futures swung to pricing a ~58–60% chance of a 25bp rate *hike* at the Sept 16 meeting (from ~36% before). A repricing toward higher-for-longer directly threatens origination volume/refi share — RKT's most rate-sensitive driver — with the pre-meeting inflation print now the swing factor.
