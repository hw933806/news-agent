# UWM raises $2.05B, suspends dividend after $451.9M Q2 loss

UWM Holdings (UWMC) — Rocket's #1 originator rival — reported Q2 2026 (quarter
ended Jun 30) on **Aug 5, 2026**: total revenue $888.0M, **net loss $451.9M**,
adjusted EBITDA $185.9M. Loan origination volume $39.7B (flat YoY, down from
$44.9B in Q1) at a 133 bps total gain margin.

Alongside the loss, UWM announced:
- A **$2.05B strategic capital investment** from Oaktree Capital and the Ishbia
  family (via SFS Group Capital), structured as preferred equity + warrants.
- **Suspension of its common dividend** to prioritize debt reduction and
  balance-sheet strength (the yield had blown out above ~19%).
- A **$400M rights offering** to Class A shareholders (record date Oct 2, 2026;
  commences Oct 5; expires Nov 12, 2026).

Read-through for RKT: the #2 originator is taking outside rescue capital, cutting
its dividend, and diluting shareholders to shore up the balance sheet in a
high-rate (30-yr ~6.7%) origination environment. Marks the competitive/distress
backdrop against which Rocket reports its own Q2 the next day (Aug 6). Frames
gain-on-sale margin pressure and volume weakness as industry-wide, not RKT-specific.
