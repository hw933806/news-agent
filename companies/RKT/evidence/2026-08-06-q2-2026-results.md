# Rocket Q2 2026: most profitable quarter in four years post-integration

Rocket Companies (RKT) reported Q2 2026 (quarter ended Jun 30) on **Aug 6, 2026** —
the first clean quarter reflecting the fully consolidated Mr. Cooper (servicing,
closed Oct 2025) and Redfin (closed Jul 2025):

- **Total revenue, net $2.78B** (adjusted revenue $2.76B), nearly double YoY —
  reflecting the added servicing + real-estate segments.
- **GAAP net income $229M**; **adjusted net income $441M**.
- **Adjusted EBITDA $766M**.
- Management: "most profitable quarter in four years"; reached **record purchase
  and refinance market share** in "one of the toughest spring housing markets in
  years" (CEO).
- Despite the profitability, results **missed Street estimates** (Benzinga), and the
  quarter came against a high-rate backdrop (30-yr ~6.7%).

Read-through: the diversified model (origination gain-on-sale + recurring servicing
UPB from Mr. Cooper + Redfin lead-gen) is showing its intended counter-cyclical
earnings stability — servicing income cushions weak origination volume. Contrasts
with #2 originator UWM, which took a $2.05B rescue recap and suspended its dividend
the day prior (see 2026-08-05-uwm-recapitalization.md). Establishes the post-M&A
baseline for RKT earnings power.
