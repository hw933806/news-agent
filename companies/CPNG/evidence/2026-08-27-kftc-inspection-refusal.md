# Coupang refuses KFTC on-site inspection, sues regulator — 2026-08-27

**Type:** Regulatory (KFTC / retail law)
**Source:** Korea Times, Seoul Economic Daily, MLex — 2026-08-25 → 2026-08-27

## What happened
- The Korea Fair Trade Commission (KFTC) sought an on-site inspection of Coupang HQ
  (planned Aug 19–28) over allegations Coupang shifted the cost of price-matching
  discounts onto suppliers — a suspected violation of the Large Retail Business Act.
- Coupang **refused** the inspection and **sued** the KFTC, arguing the Framework Act
  on Administrative Investigations requires 7 days' advance notice and that the retail
  act does not fall under the no-notice exemption. The KFTC counters it may inspect
  without notice where evidence could be destroyed.
- The KFTC attempted to inspect four times, then **withdrew empty-handed on Monday
  (Aug 24)** after learning of the lawsuit — believed to be the **first such refusal
  under Korea's retail law since the KFTC was established**, stalling the probe.
- The KFTC plans to fine Coupang for refusing the inspection and to weigh a criminal
  referral. Lawmakers are moving to close the "inspection loophole" via legislation.

## Why it matters (thesis)
- Regulatory overhang is a core CPNG concern. This is a new, distinct probe (supplier
  cost-shifting) on top of the existing KRW 162.8B search-ranking fine appeal, the
  data-breach PIPC fine, and the "same person" founder-designation dispute.
- Escalation risk: potential new fine + criminal referral + a legislative response that
  could strip platforms of inspection-notice protections going forward.
