# Warby Parker Q2 CY2026: swing to profit, gross margin expands, FY guide light

Warby Parker (WRBY) reported Q2 CY2026 (quarter ended Jun 30) on **Aug 6, 2026**:

- **Revenue $235.5M, +9.8% YoY** — modestly **below** consensus.
- **Net income $4.6M** (vs a $1.75M net loss a year earlier), aided by an
  **$11.8M tariff-refund benefit** (one-time). GAAP EPS **$0.04**, in line.
- **Gross margin 57.9%** (gross profit $136.5M) vs 53.0% a year earlier — meaningful
  ~490 bps expansion.
- **337 retail stores** (US + Canada).
- **FY2026 revenue guidance ~$967.5M at the midpoint — ~1.3% below** analyst estimates.
- Continued to "advance preparations for an upcoming AI-powered eyewear launch"
  (the Google Android XR partnership, targeted fall 2026).

Read-through: profitability inflection and strong gross-margin expansion, but the
revenue miss + light full-year guide temper the growth story. The tariff refund
flatters GAAP profit (one-time). Key watch item remains the fall-2026 Google AI-glasses
launch, which management reaffirmed is on track in prep.
