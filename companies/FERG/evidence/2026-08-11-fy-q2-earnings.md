# Ferguson fiscal Q2 (quarter ended June 30, 2026): beat-and-raise (reported 2026-08-10)

Ferguson reported fiscal Q2 (quarter ended June 30, 2026) before the open on
Monday, Aug 10, beating on both lines and raising full-year guidance.

- **Sales:** ~$8.75–8.8B, +4.6% YoY (beat).
- **EPS:** adjusted EPS $3.39 vs $3.30 consensus; GAAP diluted EPS $3.43 (+6.9%).
- **Margins:** gross margin 31.0% (−20 bps); adjusted operating margin 10.7%.
- **Segment read:** non-residential led at +8%, driven by large capital projects;
  residential returned to growth — the key positive versus a soft-housing backdrop.
- **Raised FY26 guidance:** mid-single-digit net sales growth; adjusted operating
  margin 9.5–9.8%; capex $375–425M.
- **M&A / capital allocation:** reaffirmed the signed definitive agreement to
  acquire FWI Holdings ("FloWorks", ~$1.6B, announced 2026-07-13) plus five
  bolt-ons closed in the quarter; $202M of buybacks in the quarter.

Read-through: the print confirms Ferguson's margin resilience and a demand mix
still carried by non-residential/large capital projects while residential begins
to inflect positively — a constructive setup even as 30-yr mortgage rates sit near
a 5-month high (~6.6%). Post-print, shares +4.2% (Aug 10) and the aggregate 12-month
analyst target nudged up to ~$288 (from ~$286), consensus "Buy". Watch individual
sell-side notes still trickling in and the FloWorks close (expected Q3 2026).
